# 🚗 Interactive Car Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

**Transforming car sales data into actionable business insights through interactive Excel dashboarding, KPI reporting, and year-over-year performance analysis.**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Car Sales Dashboard built with Microsoft Excel** to analyze vehicle sales performance across manufacturers, months, years, and customer demographics.

The dashboard transforms raw sales records into a consolidated, executive-friendly reporting tool, enabling users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), compare annual sales performance, identify monthly trends, and evaluate manufacturer-level performance.

By integrating PivotTables, PivotCharts, interactive slicers, calculated metrics, and variance analysis, the project demonstrates how Excel can be used to develop dynamic business intelligence solutions that support informed decision-making.

## 🔍 Key Insights from the Dashboard

### 1. Strong year-over-year sales growth

Total sales value increased from **$300.34 million in 2022 to $371.19 million in 2023**, representing an increase of approximately **$70.84 million, or 23.59%**.

This indicates improved sales value performance in 2023 compared with the previous year.

### 2. Every month recorded higher sales value in 2023

The monthly comparison table shows that sales value in 2023 exceeded the corresponding month in 2022 throughout the year.

This consistent pattern suggests that the annual improvement was not confined to a single month but was reflected across all 12 monthly comparisons.

### 3. December was the strongest month by combined sales value

December recorded the highest combined sales value across the two years, at **$99.27 million**, followed by November at approximately **$96.98 million** and September at **$93.64 million**.

These results highlight the importance of examining monthly performance when evaluating sales cycles and planning inventory, marketing, and resource allocation.

### 4. Chevrolet led the displayed manufacturer rankings

Chevrolet recorded the highest displayed sales volume, with **1,819 cars sold**, and the highest displayed total sales value, at **$47,655,265**.

Ford followed closely in total sales value, recording approximately **$47.23 million**. This comparison illustrates how manufacturer-level reporting can help identify leading brands and assess their relative contributions to overall sales.

### 5. Interactive filtering enables deeper analysis

The dashboard's slicers allow users to explore sales performance by year, gender, and manufacturer.

This functionality makes it possible to move beyond overall totals and investigate specific segments, compare filtered results, and explore how performance changes across different dimensions.

### 6. Variance analysis makes performance differences visible

The monthly variance table compares 2022 and 2023 sales values and highlights the magnitude of the differences.

For example, May recorded a positive sales-value variance of **$12,005,071**, while July recorded a positive variance of **$8,308,028**. These comparisons help identify the months with the largest year-over-year changes.

*These insights describe the patterns visible in the dashboard. They do not establish the underlying causes of sales changes.*

## ⚙️ Dashboard Features

### KPI summary cards
- Total number of cars sold.
- Grand total sales value.
- Separate sales-value summaries for 2022 and 2023.

### Monthly sales trend analysis
- Line chart comparing monthly sales values across both years.
- Side-by-side examination of monthly performance.
- Visual identification of higher- and lower-performing periods.

### Year-over-year variance reporting
- Monthly sales values for each year.
- Combined monthly sales totals.
- Variance calculations to highlight performance differences.

### Manufacturer performance table
- Ranking by vehicle count.
- Total sales value by manufacturer.
- Quick identification of leading and lower-volume manufacturers.

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard demonstrates how a well-structured Excel reporting solution can help businesses:

- **Monitor performance:** Track headline sales metrics from a single view.
- **Compare results:** Evaluate annual and monthly performance differences.
- **Identify trends:** Recognize stronger sales periods and patterns across the year.
- **Assess manufacturers:** Compare vehicle brands by sales volume and value.
- **Explore segments:** Investigate filtered results without manually rebuilding reports.
- **Support planning:** Provide historical sales information that can inform inventory management, marketing reviews, and future sales planning.

The dashboard is designed as a descriptive and comparative analytics tool. It supports evidence-based discussion and planning, rather than claiming to predict future sales or explain causal relationships.
